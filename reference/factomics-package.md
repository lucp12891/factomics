# factomics: one toolbox for multi-omics factor analysis

factomics wraps several multi-omics latent factor methods (PCA, MFA,
FABIA, GFA, MOFA2, SGCCA, JIVE and the graph-guided RABFA of Zhang et
al. 2024) behind a single function,
[`run_factor_pipeline()`](https://lucp12891.github.io/factomics/reference/run_factor_pipeline.md),
and returns their results in one common format. On top of that it
provides embeddings (PCA, UMAP, t-SNE), clustering, method benchmarking,
factor interpretation and plotting, and a one-call workflow,
[`factomics()`](https://lucp12891.github.io/factomics/reference/factomics.md).
